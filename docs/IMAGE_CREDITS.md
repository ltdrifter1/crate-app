# Image credits

Planet MP3 editorial photography — Channel Surfing tiles, the Home showcase crop, and the idle Home hero.

**Not in this set:** catalog album covers from Firebase, brand lockups/mascot (original drawings), or user-uploaded art. Those are left alone.

**Not used:** Mixmag, DJ Mag, Rolling Stone, or any magazine page scans / watermarked editorial photos. Nothing is hotlinked from a magazine CDN.

All files below were downloaded from the photographer’s licensed source and committed as JPEGs under `src/assets/channels/`, `src/assets/editorial/`, and `public/channels/`.
