# Image credits

Planet MP3 editorial photography — Channel Surfing tiles, the idle Home hero, and Explore (hero, genre mosaic, mood/scene plates reuse the same stills).

**Not in this set:** catalog album covers from Firebase, brand lockups/mascot (original drawings), or user-uploaded art.

**Not used:** Mixmag, XLR8R, DJ Mag, MTV, Rolling Stone, or any magazine/TV page scans. Those titles are copyrighted. These frames are licensed documentary photographs with the same era of club, rave, and concert coverage — not generated plates.

Crops (`object-position` / baked square crops) are our only adaptations.

## Explore

Explore does not add new photographs. The editorial hero, genre mosaic, mood plates, and scene rail reuse Channel Surfing stills (`CHANNEL_ART`) and `hero-idle.jpg`.

Genre lanes without an honest documentary match (Jazz, Classical, Reggae, Latin) use catalog album-cover mosaics — never generated plates.
